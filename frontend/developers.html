Pal-Lock, next to PayPal Checkout

Your cart stays yours. You add two buttons. PayPal Checkout pays you now. Pal-Lock locks the same amount and tells your server when the money is held, released, or dropped. People who sell from TikTok or WhatsApp keep using the Funguo link. They do not need this API.

Call it the way you call PayPal

Send your API key as Authorization: Bearer. option is checkout or pal_lock. Send the buyer to approval_url. Funguo sends them back to return_url after PayPal, and posts the same news to your webhook.

What your server hears

  • payment.paid PayPal Checkout captured. You already have the money.
  • payment.held Pal-Lock placed the hold.
  • payment.released The buyer confirmed, or you called capture.
  • payment.voided The lock was dropped. The buyer was not charged.
  • payment.partial A dispute captured only part of the hold.

After a Pal-Lock is held, your server calls POST /api/v1/payments/:id/capture when the buyer confirms, or POST /api/v1/payments/:id/void to drop it.
